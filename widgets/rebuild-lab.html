Rebuild the 2×2 Lab

Each study below reports its results the way real papers do: incompletely, sideways, or at several cut-offs. Rebuild the 2×2 table you would enter into RevMan, then press Check.

How to work

  1. Find the number with and without SBI in the analysis (D and ND).
  2. Work out TP, FP, FN and TN. Round to whole infants.
  3. Recalculate the published percentages from your table. They must come back.

Enter cells in the RevMan order: TP, FP, FN, TN. All five studies are illustrative, constructed for teaching.

Answers to the Check button come from the same illustrative dataset used in the slides and the other labs.
