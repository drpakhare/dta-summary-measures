What the session covers

  1. The 2×2 table as the unit of data in a diagnostic accuracy review
  2. Sensitivity and specificity, and why they vary with the spectrum of patients
  3. Predictive values and prevalence
  4. Likelihood ratios and post-test probability
  5. The diagnostic odds ratio and what it hides
  6. Cut-offs, ROC curves and the threshold effect across studies
  7. Rebuilding a 2×2 table from what a paper reports
  8. Decisions for the protocol and the extraction form, linked to QUADAS-3
